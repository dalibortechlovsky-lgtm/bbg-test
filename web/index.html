          How to prepare the event ledger

          Use ISO dates (YYYY-MM-DD) and unique event_id values. Sequence orders fills on the same date. For each account/security/currency, add one OPENING record before its trades: signed quantity, historical average cost price (including fees), multiplier and zero fees. Use zero quantity/price for a genuinely new position. BUY/SELL quantity is positive; fees are nonnegative, in the same currency. For bonds priced in percent, supply multiplier 0.01 only after verifying nominal units; for options supply the verified contract multiplier.

          INCOME uses net amount. CASHFLOW is an external deposit (positive) or withdrawal (negative), never a trade or dividend. VALUATION uses complete closing account value, including cash, in the stated currency. Leave security/quantity/price/multiplier/fees blank for these records. Consecutive dates produce daily P/L; gaps produce period P/L. Separate accounts/currencies are never silently netted. Repeated event IDs are ignored; conflicting IDs reject the entire file. Edit the source CSV before import; corrections to stored records need a reviewed adjustment workflow.

            Risk input requirements

            Copy position_id from the holdings identifiers below. kind is european_call, european_put or future. Supply verified underlying, currency, multiplier, spot (futures quote for future), timezone-aware expiry and observed_at, and verified=yes. Options also require strike, volatility (0.25 means 25%), rate and dividend_yield as decimal fractions. American/exotic/structured contracts are not supported. Position quantities come from the dated UBS snapshot. Updating PX_LAST does not refresh volatility or other risk inputs; upload a new verified input set.
